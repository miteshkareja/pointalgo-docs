Understanding GARCH Volatility in Nifty

Volatility isn't just "risk"—it's data. In the Indian markets, specifically the Nifty 50, volatility clustering is a phenomenon where high-volatility events tend to follow other high-volatility events.

The GARCH Logic

At PointAlgo, we utilize GARCH (Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity) models to filter our trade entries. When the model detects a variance spike that exceeds the 2-standard deviation threshold, our "Quant Engine" tightens stops or waits for a mean-reversion signal.
