The PointAlgo Journey

2013

The Genesis

Founder Mitesh Mistry begins his trading journey, sparking an in-depth, decade-long pursuit of market mechanics and price action behavior.

2017

Shift to Quants

A deep dive into advanced technical analysis and quantitative mechanics begins, mapping the gap between retail tools and real market movement.

2022

Trial & Error

Commenced heavy development of custom indicators and strategy creation. Countless hours spent building, testing, and refining early-stage frameworks.

2023

Research & Meta-Modeling

PointAlgo research officially begins. The systems shift from basic technicals to advanced metaethical modeling utilizing Linear Algebra, Probability, Statistics, and Geometry.

2024

Real-Time Implication

Theoretical math becomes compiled code. PointAlgo starts deploying proprietary models into real-time market data to test latency, accuracy, and execution.

2025

Enterprise Deployment

Custom algorithms and bespoke infrastructure successfully deployed to major Indian brokers and large-scale financial enterprises.

2026

Retail Market Launch

PointAlgo opens its doors to the public. We start providing retail traders—who are historically unaware of quant-level indicators—access to institutional-grade systems.
