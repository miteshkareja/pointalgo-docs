Refund & Cancellation

Last Updated: April 2026

Due to the digital nature of our intellectual property and trading tools, please read our strict policy regarding cancellations and refunds before making a purchase.

1. Cancellation Policy

You have full control over your subscription. You may cancel your subscription at any time.

  • If you cancel your subscription, you will not be billed for any future billing cycles.
  • Your access to the premium indicators on TradingView will remain active until the end of your currently paid billing period.
  • Cancellations can be processed through your account dashboard or by contacting our support team directly.

Because our products are digital, intangible goods (TradingView scripts, quantitative logic, and proprietary code algorithms), they cannot be "returned" once accessed. Therefore, we do not offer refunds once an indicator or subscription is sold and access is granted.

We highly encourage all users to thoroughly review our free documentation, strategy logic, and open-source toolkit before committing to a premium purchase. By completing a transaction on PointAlgo, you explicitly agree to waive any right to a refund for digital access.

For custom solutions, the refund and cancellation policy is strictly dictated by the terms provided at the time of sale. These specific terms will be outlined in the formal proposal or invoice sent to you via our official email address (hello@pointalgo.com) prior to the commencement of the project.

If you believe there has been a billing error (e.g., duplicate charge), please contact our support desk immediately. Fraudulent chargebacks filed against valid digital goods deliveries will result in a permanent ban from PointAlgo and the immediate revocation of all indicator access.
